When everything's ready, press on Google Trash It!

4. SEE THE RESULTS

After a certain amount of time, you should see something different at the output.

It is possible that the output is close, or the same as the input, but the higher the amount of iterations is, or the longer the input is, the more chance you will encounter differences.

Please note: The Demo does not translate those sentences/words in real-time. They are just results from Google Trash's tests from Team Geek, its creator.

To simplify, Google Trash does not work that fast: It is Google Trash's actual translations pre-written.
